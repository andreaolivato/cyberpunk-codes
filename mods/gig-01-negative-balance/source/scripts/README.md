# Negative Balance

A story gig for Cyberpunk 2077, adapted from the comic of the same name.
An accounts clerk notices people dying on schedule, and calls the wrong merc.

## Requirements

RED4ext, ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware, redscript.

Cyber Engine Tweaks is NOT required. Neither is Audioware or mod_settings.

## Voices

Every character is voiced with AI-generated speech (ElevenLabs). No performance
by any real actor was cloned or reused, except one line of Nix's that points at
his own shipped recording. Subtitles are on every line.

## Good to know

The gig switches on the five doors into the Arasaka office in Santo Domingo. The
base game ships them switched off until a later main-story mission, so without
this the gig would send you to a building you cannot enter. They are switched on
while the gig is running and never switched back: a mod that closes a door behind
you can shut you in. If you play that mission later, those doors will already be
working. Nothing else in the compound is changed and the vehicle gates are
untouched.
